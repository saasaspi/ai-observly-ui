**Why:** The user said, “Remove '—' from the home page” and “Substitute '—' with ','” because they do not want the page to look AI-generated.

**How to apply:** Keep new homepage copy consistent with this requirement; do not extend this punctuation change to dashboard or blog article content without a request.
