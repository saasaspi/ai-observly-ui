## 1. Founders — `/use-cases/founders`

**Meta Title:** AI Cost & Margin Tracking for Founders | AI Observly
**Meta Description:** See which customers and features are quietly burning your AI margins — before they burn your runway. Built for non-technical founders, live in minutes.

**H1:** Know exactly which customers are eating your AI margins

**Subhead:** You added AI to your product. Your OpenAI or Anthropic bill went up. AI Observly shows you exactly who's driving it — and whether they're still profitable.

**The Problem**
- **One invoice, zero answers.** You can see your total LLM spend, but not which customers or plans are actually driving it.
- **Margin-negative customers hide in plain sight.** A customer on your $49/month plan can easily cost you $80/month in AI usage — and you won't know until it's a pattern, not an incident.
- **Pricing is a guess.** Without per-customer or per-plan cost data, every pricing decision — raising a tier, adding usage limits, sunsetting a plan — is based on a hunch, not a number.

**How AI Observly Helps**
- **Per-customer cost attribution.** See exactly what each customer costs you in AI spend, right next to their plan and revenue — no spreadsheet required.
- **Plan & pricing profitability.** Instantly see which pricing tiers are actually making you money and which ones are quietly subsidized by your other customers.
- **Built for founders, not engineers.** One lightweight integration — no proxy, no stored API keys — gets you live data the same day, no dev sprint needed.

**CTA:** Stop guessing which customers are worth keeping. → **Start monitoring now**

## 2. Product Managers — `/use-cases/product-managers`

**Meta Title:** Feature-Level AI Cost & ROI Data for Product Managers | AI Observly
**Meta Description:** Tie every AI feature to its true cost-to-serve. Prioritize your roadmap with real margin data instead of gut feel.

**H1:** Ship AI features you know are worth building

**Subhead:** Usage graphs tell you a feature is popular. AI Observly tells you what that popularity actually costs — and whether it's worth doubling down on.

**The Problem**
- **Adoption without economics.** You can see a feature's usage climbing, but not what each use costs to serve — so you can't tell if growth is a win or a slow leak.
- **Roadmap calls run on opinion.** Without per-feature cost data, prioritization conversations default to whoever argues loudest, not what the numbers say.
- **No line back to the customer.** When a feature's costs spike, it's hard to tell whether it's one heavy user, a whole segment, or a genuine usage pattern worth designing around.

**How AI Observly Helps**
- **Per-feature cost & margin.** See what each AI feature costs to run — broken down by customer and plan — so usage and cost live in the same view.
- **Cost-aware prioritization.** Bring real cost-to-serve numbers into roadmap discussions, not just usage counts.
- **Customer-level context.** Trace a feature's cost back to the specific accounts driving it, so you know if it's a segment worth building for or an outlier to manage.

**CTA:** Bring cost data to your next roadmap review. → **Start monitoring now**

## 4. Engineering — `/use-cases/engineering`

**Meta Title:** Lightweight AI Cost Attribution for Engineering Teams | AI Observly
**Meta Description:** One fire-and-forget endpoint. No proxy in your request path. No stored API keys. Get per-customer cost attribution without building it yourself.

**H1:** Cost attribution without building it yourself

**Subhead:** Product and finance keep asking what an AI feature costs per customer. AI Observly answers that without you having to build a logging pipeline to get there.

**The Problem**
- **"What does this cost per customer?" has no easy answer.** Getting there yourself means custom logging, a data pipeline to aggregate it, and someone to maintain both.
- **Most cost-tracking tools want to sit in the request path.** Proxying every LLM call adds a moving part and a new failure mode to something that already works.
- **Provider credentials become another thing to secure.** Tools that need your API keys to work are one more surface to worry about.

**How AI Observly Helps**
- **Fire-and-forget usage-report endpoint.** Report usage from your existing code with a single call — no proxy, nothing sitting between your app and the LLM provider.
- **No API keys stored, ever.** AI Observly never touches your provider credentials — it only sees the usage data you choose to send.
- **Ship it once.** A lightweight integration you can add in one sitting, not a sprint — and you're done answering the cost question by hand.

**CTA:** Get cost visibility without the infra project. → **Start monitoring now**

## 5. Finance & Ops — `/use-cases/finance`

**Meta Title:** AI Cost Data for Unit Economics & Forecasting | AI Observly for Finance & Ops
**Meta Description:** Turn unpredictable LLM spend into real per-customer and per-plan cost data — the missing input for unit economics and margin models.

**H1:** Turn your AI bill into real unit economics

**Subhead:** Usage-based AI pricing makes the vendor invoice a moving target. AI Observly gives you the per-customer and per-plan cost data to model it properly instead of reacting to it.

**The Problem**
- **AI/LLM spend is the hardest line item to forecast.** It scales with usage, not headcount or seats, so last month's bill tells you very little about next month's.
- **Contribution margin models are missing a piece.** Without per-customer AI cost, any margin or unit economics model built on top of your SaaS revenue is incomplete.
- **Overruns are discovered after the fact.** By the time a cost problem shows up on the provider invoice, the quarter it affected is already closed.

**How AI Observly Helps**
- **Per-customer & per-plan cost data.** The input your unit economics and contribution margin models are currently missing, without asking engineering to pull it manually.
- **Plan-level profitability.** See which pricing tiers are structurally profitable and which are being propped up by your other plans.
- **A shared source of truth.** The same cost numbers finance, product, and founders are looking at — no more reconciling spreadsheets before a board meeting.

**CTA:** Give your margin models the missing input. → **Start monitoring now**
