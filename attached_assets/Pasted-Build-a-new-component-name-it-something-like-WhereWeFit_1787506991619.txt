What it shows

A scatter/quadrant chart plotting AI Observly against 8 competitors on two axes:

X-axis (horizontal): who the tool is built for — from "Developers & Engineering Teams" (left) to "Non-Technical Founders" (right)
Y-axis (vertical): what problem it solves — from "Observability & Debugging" (bottom) to "Business Profitability & Pricing" (top)

AI Observly sits alone in the top-right corner. A dashed divider splits the chart into four quadrants, three of which are labeled directly on the chart: Founder-First Profitability (top-right, where AI Observly sits), Engineering-Led Profitability (top-left), and Developer Observability (bottom-left). The bottom-right quadrant is intentionally empty/unlabeled.

Required interactions
Hover any point → a tooltip appears showing the tool's name, category, and one-line description.
Tap a point on mobile → same tooltip, toggles open/closed on repeat tap.
Click a legend chip (AI Observly / Per-customer cost tools / LLM observability / Cloud & AI FinOps) → dims every point not in that category. Click again to restore. Multiple chips can be active at once.
The "AI Observly" point has a pulsing ring around it to draw the eye — respect prefers-reduced-motion and disable the pulse for users who have that set.
On narrow/mobile screens, the rotated vertical axis label should switch to a plain horizontal caption above the chart instead of trying to rotate in a cramped space (see the reference implementation's mobile handling — it already solves this).
